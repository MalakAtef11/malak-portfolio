CASE STUDY 01 / 07 · 2026

NOURISH.

Healthy-food commerce with an interactive bowl builder, creator recipes and subscriptions.

Product strategy · System analysisWorking baseline · not presented as launched
01CASE
FILE✳
01 — THE PROBLEM

Where it starts.

Conventional food ordering separates ingredient-level customization, nutrition visibility and ordering. The dossier explores combining them without compromising pricing, operational accuracy or privacy.

02 — THE APPROACH

How it works.

A modular product blueprint connecting a gamified bowl builder, nutrition estimates, creator recipes, subscriptions, kitchen operations and an administration portal.

MY CONTRIBUTION / EVIDENCE

System analysis and software requirements documentation; the source explicitly states this is an architecture-ready working baseline requiring approval, not proof of a deployed product.
