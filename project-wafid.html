SYSTEMS ANALYSIS CONTRIBUTION / 2026

WAFID.

An international-student services platform concept covering applications, accommodation and helpful local information. My contribution was to analysis, requirements and documentation — not software implementation.

Analysis onlyRequirementsDocumentationTeam project
PROJECT02ARCHIVE
01 / PROJECT CONTEXT

One experience for student needs.

International students often need to navigate admissions information, document requirements, housing and nearby services across different channels. WAFID explored how those journeys could be organized into one coherent platform.

My role focused on understanding these processes, translating them into documented requirements and flows, and contributing to the project presentation.

MY ROLE

Systems analysis & documentation

This case study showcases the thinking and documentation contribution. I did not build or program the platform and do not claim the implementation as mine.

02 / AREAS OF ANALYSIS

The workflows we considered.

01

An end-to-end admission/application journey and related document handling requirements.

02
