PROJECT OVERVIEW / JAVA

Run. Fight. Survive.

Crimson Quest is a side-scrolling action platformer I created using Java. The player moves through a world of platforms and gaps, fights enemies and collects health to keep going. The gameplay has the feel of a classic Mario-style platform adventure, but with combat and a visible health system.

HOW TO PLAY ↗

Move and jump across the platforms, attack enemies and collect health along the way. Watch the health bar and make it through the level without getting defeated.
